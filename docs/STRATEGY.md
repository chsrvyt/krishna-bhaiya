# Campaign Strategy: LatAm Mining Outbound (1 page)

**Goal:** Book discovery calls with Head of Ops, VP HSE, Site Directors at lithium, copper, iron ore operators.
**Angle:** Autonomous drone inspection replacing contracted crews at hazardous 24/7 sites. Proof: Anglo American, Shell, CSX.

## 1. Sequence (21 days, 7 touches, 3 channels)

| Day | Channel | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Email | Signal-led intro (company-specific hook + 1 proof + soft ask) |
| 3 | LinkedIn | Connection request, no pitch, one line referencing the same signal |
| 6 | Email | New angle by persona (Ops: cost/cycle time; HSE: exposure) + short case-style proof |
| 10 | LinkedIn / WhatsApp* | Short voice note or message with a relevant asset (1-min site-inspection clip) |
| 14 | Email | Peer insight: what similar mining sites inspect with drones; ask "who owns this?" |
| 18 | Phone (optional, Tier A) | Call + voicemail referencing earlier email |
| 21 | Email | Breakup: permission-based, offer to route to the right person |

*WhatsApp only where the contact has opted in/publicly lists it; LatAm norm, but respect consent and local rules.

## 2. Trigger logic (fast-track to top of queue)
- **Job change** into Ops/HSE/Site role (new leaders review vendors in first 90 days)
- **Expansion/capex news:** new project, ramp-up, permit approval
- **Safety incident or regulator action** at a site (handle with tact, no opportunism)
- **Press/tech signals:** autonomy, remote ops center, drone/AI mentions, ESG reports
- **Events:** speaking or attending Expomin, Exponor, Mining Indaba-type events
- **Competitor/contractor change:** contract tenders for inspection/survey services
Fast-track = move to Day-1 touch within 48h with the trigger as the hook.

## 3. Personalization at scale (10 → 200 accounts)
- **Tiering:** Tier A (top 20): full agent research + human review of every email. Tier B (next 60): agent research + sampled review (20%). Tier C (rest): signal-templated by persona, still fact-sourced.
- **Source-or-silence + QA gate** (Stage 5) blocks unsupported claims automatically.
- Weekly human audit of 10 random emails; failures feed prompt updates.
- Cache research per account; refresh on trigger events only.
- Reply data trains hook rankings (which signal types get replies).

## 4. Success metrics (first 2 weeks)
| Metric | Healthy | Broken |
|---|---|---|
| Deliverability (bounce) | <3% | >5% → fix list quality/domain warm-up |
| Open rate (directional) | 45%+ | <30% → subject/deliverability |
| Reply rate | 5-8% | <2% → message/targeting |
| Positive reply rate | 2-3% | ~0% after 100 sends → ICP/persona wrong |
| Meetings booked | 1-2 per 100 contacts | none after 150 → offer/CTA issue |
| Wrong-person referrals | track | high → persona map off |
Leading indicators: LinkedIn acceptance rate (>25%), referral replies ("talk to X").

## 5. First A/B test (week 1)
**Hypothesis:** Opening with a *company-specific operational signal* gets higher positive replies than opening with *peer proof* (Anglo American).
- **A:** Signal-first hook, proof in line 3. **B:** Proof-first, signal in line 2.
- Same persona, same subject style, 40 contacts per arm. Primary metric: positive reply rate. Why: tests whether our edge is research depth (scalable via agent) or brand proof, which shapes how much to invest in per-account research.
