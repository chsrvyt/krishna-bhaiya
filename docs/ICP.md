# ICP Model (anchored on SQM)

## Why SQM is the anchor
SQM (Sociedad Química y Minera de Chile) is a large-scale, high-altitude, 24/7 operator in the Atacama (lithium brine, potassium, iodine) with sprawling open-air footprints (salt flats, evaporation ponds, plants). Large areas, remote/hazardous conditions, and heavy reliance on contractors make it a strong fit for autonomous inspection. *The agent must verify all of these from current public sources in Stage 0; this is the hypothesis, not the evidence.*

## Fit criteria and weights (score 0-100)

| Criterion | Weight | What counts as a signal |
|---|---|---|
| Operational scale | 20 | Multi-site, large footprint (ponds, pits, tailings, stockpiles, haul roads) |
| 24/7 hazardous operations | 20 | Open-pit/brine ops, remote, altitude, heat, tailings/pond inspection |
| Contractor dependence | 15 | Public mentions of contractor-heavy ops, outsourced inspection/survey |
| Geography | 10 | Chile / Argentina / Peru / Brazil / Mexico etc. (Atacama + Lithium Triangle weighted highest) |
| Commodity match | 10 | Lithium, copper, iron ore |
| Technology/innovation signals | 15 | Digital mining, autonomy, remote ops centers, drone/AI mentions, ESG tech investment |
| Expansion / capex trigger | 10 | New project, expansion, permitting, ramp-up in last 12-18 months |

**Tiering:** 80+ = Tier A (full personalization), 60-79 = Tier B, <60 = excluded.

## Disqualifiers
- Small/junior explorers with no producing operations
- Operations already publicly committed to an exclusive competing drone provider (flag, don't auto-exclude)
- No identifiable operations leadership in public sources

## Seed hypotheses for the agent to test (NOT outputs; verify each)
Lithium: Albemarle (Chile), Arcadium Lithium/Rio Tinto, Sigma Lithium, Minera Exar / Lithium Americas, Eramet (Argentina).
Copper: Codelco, Escondida (BHP), Antofagasta Minerals (Los Pelambres, Centinela), Collahuasi, Southern Copper, Cerro Verde, Antamina, Las Bambas.
Iron ore: Vale (Brazil), CSN Mineração, CAP Minería (Chile), Anglo American Minas-Rio.

*Note: Anglo American is a FlytBase customer, so treat as a "land and expand" or reference-proof account, not cold.*
