# Stage 3: Account Research Brief

**Input:** one account from `accounts.json` + its contacts
**Tools:** web_search, web_fetch

**Task:** Write a research brief using only real, citable public data.

**Sections (required):**
1. **Snapshot:** what they operate, where, commodity, scale (sourced numbers only)
2. **Recent news (last 12 months):** 3-5 items with date + URL
3. **Operational footprint:** sites, remote/hazardous characteristics (altitude, brine ponds, pits, tailings, haul roads, 24/7 shifts)
4. **Technology & expansion signals:** automation, digital, drones, remote ops, capex, new projects (sourced)
5. **Contractor dependence:** evidence of contracted inspection/survey/maintenance labor (sourced); mark “inferred” if not explicit
6. **FlytBase angle:** the 1-2 sharpest, specific connections between this account's situation and autonomous drone inspection. Include which customer proof (Anglo American, Shell, CSX…) is most credible for THIS account and why.
7. **Per-persona hooks:** one hook each for Ops / HSE / Site Director
8. **Gaps:** what you couldn't verify

**Format:** Markdown. Every bullet with a fact ends with `[source: URL, date]`.
**Rules:** Label each statement `FACT` (sourced) or `INFERENCE` (reasoned, unsourced). Emails may only use `FACT` items as claims.
